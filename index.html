Today

Your day at a glance.
No coach, no feed.

Open Statly and your day is right there, on one calm screen. Nothing to scroll past, nothing telling you how to feel about it.

Privacy

Your health data
stays on your phone.

Statly reads from Health Connect, the place on Android where your tracker shares its data. You choose exactly what Statly may read, and you can change that any time.

  • Stays on your phoneNo server and no account. Your readings are never uploaded.
  • Read-onlyStatly only reads. It never writes to or changes your health data.
  • Never soldYour data isn't sold, shared or used to profile you.
  • No adsNot now, not later. You pay for the app, so you aren't the product.

FAQ

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Write to abdulhady.aariyan@gmail.com.

Does it work with my watch?

Statly works with any watch, ring or tracker whose app shares data to Health Connect. Turn on sharing in your tracker's app once, then connect Statly. If your tracker doesn't share to Health Connect, Statly can't see its data.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 9 or newer. On Android 14 and later, Health Connect is built in. On Android 9 to 13 it's a free app you install once; Statly shows you how.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your phone. Statly reads your steps, sleep, heart rate and exercise from Health Connect, shows them, and keeps what it needs for trends and badges on your device. There is no account and no Statly server holding your health data, and it is never sold. You can remove Statly's access in Health Connect at any time, and uninstalling removes what Statly kept. The privacy policy has the details.

Is there an AI coach?

No, on purpose. Statly shows your numbers clearly and leaves the thinking to you.

Is Statly a medical app?

No. Statly displays the data your tracker records. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and its numbers are only as accurate as your tracker's. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.
