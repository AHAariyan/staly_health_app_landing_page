How it works

Your phone is enough.
Just one permission.

Install Statly, allow "Physical activity", and put the phone in your pocket. There is nothing to pair, no other app to install and no account to make.

  • Steps against your goal, counted by your phone's motion sensor all day, with what's left in plain words: "6,400 to go, about a 65-minute walk."
  • Last night's sleep, estimated: when you went to bed, when you woke and how long you slept, worked out from how still the phone was. Always labelled as an estimate.
  • Active minutes from brisk walking, plus estimated distance and calories from your steps.
  • Streaks, badges and trends that build up on their own as you walk.

Optional

Have a watch?
Bring it along.

You don't need one. But if you already wear a watch or tracker, Statly can import its sleep stages, heart rate and workouts through Health Connect. Turn it on in Settings whenever you like.

  • Read-only. Statly never writes to or changes anything in Health Connect.
  • No double counting. On days your watch has steps, Statly uses its count instead of the phone's.
  • Easy to undo. Turn the import off and Statly deletes its imported copy. Your phone keeps counting either way.

Privacy

Counted and kept
on your phone.

Statly measures with the phone you already carry, and what it measures never leaves it. You can switch the permission off any time in Android's settings.

  • Stays on your phoneYour steps and sleep are never uploaded. No server holds them.
  • No accountNo sign-in, no email, no name. Open it and go.
  • Never soldYour data isn't sold, shared or used to profile you.
  • No adsNot now, not later. You pay for the app, so you aren't the product.

Anonymous usage stats and crash reports help fix bugs; they never include your health data, and you can turn them off in Settings. Details in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Write to abdulhady.aariyan@gmail.com.

Do I need a watch?

No. Statly counts your steps and estimates your sleep with your phone alone. If you already wear a watch or tracker, you can optionally import its sleep stages, heart rate and workouts through Health Connect, in Settings.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 9 or newer. Statly uses the phone's own motion sensor, so there's nothing else to install. It asks for one permission, which Android calls "Physical activity".

How is sleep estimated?

From how still your phone is at night, worked out on the phone itself. Statly shows when you went to bed, when you woke and how long you slept, and always marks it as an estimate. It works best if your phone stays near you overnight. It can't see sleep stages; for those, you can import sleep from a watch.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your phone. Your steps, sleep estimates and anything you import are stored only on your device and are never uploaded, sold or used for ads. There is no account. Statly sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports that never include health data, and you can turn them off in Settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes everything Statly kept. The privacy policy has the details.

Is Statly a medical app?

No. Statly is for everyday motivation. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and its sleep, distance and calorie numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.
